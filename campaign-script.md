# Greenhouse Nine · Campaign Script

This file is generated from the game's own tables (`CAMP`, `INTRO`, `STORY`) plus the hand-written story notes below. Regenerate it whenever dialogue changes. Mission 2 is a design plan, not built yet unless the game's mission list shows it as available.

## World and lore

- **Year 2687.** Humanity lives in a golden age. Robots and AI are everywhere, and the military base on Thalassa Station grows farms for the Europa colonies.
- **The Blade.** A sword-shaped relic the size of a moon has hung off Jupiter since 2214. It gave humanity magic-grade technology. (Placeholder name.)
- **The Dark.** Something beyond the stars answered the Blade's light. It wants the sword. (Placeholder name.) Its signal inside machines is called **the Undertow**.
- **Phase 1: the machines.** The Dark hacks every military and civilian AI: Mars, Ceres, then Thalassa. Drones, repair frames and loaders turn.
- **Phase 2: the Dark's own species** (planned). Large armoured organic creatures with advanced armour and dark magic, in several types, invade Europa by land.
- **The Wellmonger.** The Dark's general, who leads the invasion. Named in Mission 1 stage 1 by Fleet Command. A boss for later; its design is still to be decided.
- **Meridian Station.** The human group-up station in orbit over Europa, where Fleet Command is.
- **Halcyon.** A friendly ship that is being boarded when Mission 2 reaches it; the allies are on board.

## Cast

- **The player.** The last soldier awake on the farm deck. A class (Bulwark, Strider or Grafter) carrying six guns and three supers.
- **Rowan Adeyemi.** Deck botanist. Eleven years growing wheat for the Europa colonies; his daughter Imani is in Cryo-farm Four on Europa. Seals himself in the seed vault, then joins the player as a computer-controlled ally with a grow-lab laser called the pruner, carrying the seed case. He escapes with the player.
- **Lattice.** The station AI, taken by the Dark. Low, slow, patient voice.
- **Fleet Command.** The radio voice from Meridian Station.

### Level 1 ending

After the Matriarch falls and the closing lines finish, a "Level 1 complete" screen reads aloud: Thalassa holds, Rowan has the seed case, the shuttle is waiting in the hangar for the flight to Meridian Station. The player can keep fighting endless stages or (when built) continue to Mission 2.

## Mission 2 · Flight to Meridian Station (design plan)

The player pilots a shuttle. Speed reaches **500 mph** with the afterburner. Weapons built into the shuttle: **machine guns**, **homing missiles**, and a **heavy mass-accelerator cannon**.

- **2-1 Launch.** Leave the hangar, meet the first Dark interceptors outside, learn the three weapons.
- **2-2 Rescue.** Fly to the besieged ship Halcyon, clear its attackers, then dock to pick up allies (two wingmen join).
- **2-3 Run to Meridian.** Fly the long run with ship encounters, break through a blockade, and dock at Meridian Station.

The finale hands off to the ground invasion of Europa (Mission 3, not designed yet).

## Mission 3 · The Wellmonger's Fleet (built, v91)

**3-1 First Strike.** Kestrel launches from Meridian's Hangar 0 carrying the last Nova warhead and jumps to Europa, where
the Wellmonger's command ship (10.5 km, the Dark's black blade-hull design) holds station. At 7 km the warhead goes in: a
direct hit on the shield at full yield, and nothing. Then the fleet jumps in through tearing portals, cruisers and frigates
bursting out on top of you. Fleet Command: their shield lets slow traffic through. Rowan spots a lit bay on the starboard
side; you cross the shield under 200 mph and land.

**3-2 The Heart.** On foot with Rowan through the command ship: the bay, the spine, the barracks, the conduit hall, the
throat, and the heart, the shield generator. Rowan carries the charge; you hold the room while it is set (hold E), then
two and a half minutes to get back to the Kestrel with everything aboard coming for you.

**3-3 Shot Down.** Out of the bay with 25 seconds on the clock. The command ship goes up: the shield collapses and the
hull breaks in three. Nine interceptors on your tail; Fleet Command can't reach you and sends you toward Europa. The
second ace, Sable, arrives, plays with you, and puts a missile into the engines. The Kestrel spirals down over Europa.

## Mission 4 · Europa (built, v91; the story stops here)

**4-1 Crash Landing.** No engines and no air: only the retro thrusters (hold W, 9 s of fuel) and the nose. The Kestrel
hits the ice of a long plain between ridges and spires, skids, and stops. Rowan: they are alive; Imani is somewhere on
this moon; the Dark is coming down after them. To be continued.
